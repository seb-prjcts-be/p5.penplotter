# p5.penplotter v0.3.1

2026-10-05

Updates the bundled vanilla core to 131efc79ebf9341dcc13dced550d6877e831ba6b. DrawCore plot commands have a separate commandTimeoutMs, default 120000 ms; connection and status checks keep short timeouts.

Normal Stop lets accepted motion finish, raises the pen and confirms Idle. It is a controlled stop, not an immediate emergency stop. Errors and emergencyStop request feed-hold, which does not guarantee penlift. There is no automatic resume, unlock, homing or coordinate reset.

Recalculates automatic paper placement when the connected driver selects the configured bed. Recorded geometry follows the new position without rescaling. Explicit paper and drawing offsets remain unchanged. The final plan is prepared after connection.

Examples use A4 and fit the chosen sheet, with other paper and A3 H settings shown as comments. The calibration sheet keeps its physical scale with a 5 mm A4 margin. Sessions and the servo-command example are labelled EBB only; DrawCore sessions and multiple pens remain unsupported.

Validation covers the adapter, pinned core, simulated DrawCore controlled stop and placement, reproducible module/classic bundles, instance startup and all browser examples. Small DrawCore drawings and paper placement have been physically confirmed. The newest stop with penlift and larger drawings still need physical tests.

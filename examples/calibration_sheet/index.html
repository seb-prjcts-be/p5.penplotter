Calibration sheet

What your pen does on your paper, in millimetres. A 100 mm ruler to check the scale, hatch and cross-hatch scales from 6 to 0.8 mm, stipple densities, circles from 2 to 40 mm, and line pairs from 0.3 to 2 mm apart. Only physical variables: nothing a screen could fake. Plot it once per pen and keep the sheet.

Pen up and down

Click the sketch, then press U to raise the pen or D to lower it. The browser asks for a port on the first keypress. Use Chrome or Edge on localhost or HTTPS.

This sketch sends no XY moves and keeps the board's existing servo calibration. D leaves the pen down: press U promptly when testing over paper. If U does not lift it, switch the plotter off.

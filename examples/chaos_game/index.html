Chaos game · time is the order

Chaos game

One point, three corners. At each step the point jumps halfway to a randomly chosen corner and leaves a dot. A Sierpinski triangle emerges. The sketch prepares all 1,500 points before plotting and preserves their order, so you can watch the pattern appear on paper. The duration depends on the machine settings. Live streaming is a separate proposal and is not implemented.

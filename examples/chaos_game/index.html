Chaos game · time is the order

Chaos game

One point, three corners. Every step the point jumps halfway to a corner chosen at random and leaves a dot. After a few hundred dots the Sierpinski triangle is there; nobody drew it. The plan keeps the order of the game, so on paper the triangle appears the way it appears here: dot by dot, out of nothing, about half an hour long. On a screen this is over in a second. This is the first taste of live mode; in sheet mode it has an end.

About p5.penplotter

I wanted to try plotting a drawing from a p5 sketch, while keeping the sketch familiar to work with. Recording the shapes as they are drawn seemed a useful place to start.

How this was made

I use AI for code, tests and documentation. I test the drawings on my iDraw HSE / A2. The Molnar example has now completed its first full plot on paper.

Sebastien Vanblaere · prjcts.be

Related work

  • p5.plotSvg by Golan Levin records p5 drawings as SVG and covers a wider drawing API.
  • p5.plotterControl by Craig Fahner controls GRBL pen plotters live from p5.js.
  • p5.gysin and wave samplers: the sister libraries; the second supplies the numbers several examples here draw with.

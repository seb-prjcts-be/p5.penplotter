Eight Lines

This is a complete sketch. Paste it in the editor, or in any p5 project, and you have a drawing that can go to paper.

let plot;

function setup() {
  createCanvas(400, 250);
  plot = createPlot({ x: 60, y: 60, width: 120 });   // 120 mm wide, 60 mm from the corner
  plot.rect(10, 10, 380, 230);
  plot.circle(200, 125, 120);
  plot.go();                                          // ready: click the drawing
}

The editor needs the library imports and installation in index.html, once. Copy them from the start page.

change one thing and look

One Change

A number, a shape, a line. Each one shows you something the screen alone cannot.

  1. 01120 → 200

    The same drawing, 200 mm wide on paper. The screen does not change; the pen does.

  2. 02a circle → a hatch

    Replace the circle with plot.hatch([[40, 40], [360, 40], [360, 210], [40, 210]], 6): a filled shape, made of lines, because a pen has no fill.

  3. 03order → disorder

    Open the Molnár grid and change wanorde from rij / (n - 1) to 1: every row comes apart.

Supported shape calls record geometry for paper: shapes, lines and dots. translate(), rotate(), fill() and colours stay on the screen; paper uses the recorded coordinates and the pen in the holder.

put it on paper

On Paper

Four steps, the first time and every time.

  1. 01Chrome or Edge

    With the plotter on USB. Web Serial lives nowhere else.

  2. 02Home corner

    Slide the carriage to the home corner by hand, and put the paper down.

  3. 03Click your drawing

    Choose the plotter in the list that opens. It draws.

  4. 04Click again to stop

    The pen comes up, the motors let go.

Direct plotting has been tested on the iDraw HSE / A2 with EBB firmware 3.0.2. Without hardware you can preview recorded shapes and use plot.engine.exportSVG(). Use the exported file with software that supports your machine.

under the hood

Under the Hood

p5.penplotter draws supported p5 shapes and records their coordinates in millimetres. vanilla.penplotter supplies cleanup, route planning, previews, file exports and the machine driver. It can also be used without p5.js.

Eight lines

Copy the imports from Setup into index.html. Put this drawing in sketch.js.

let plot;

function setup() {
  createCanvas(400, 250);
  plot = createPlot({ paper: "A4", margin: 12, width: 120 });   // centred on A4 paper
  plot.rect(10, 10, 380, 230);
  plot.circle(200, 125, 120);
  plot.go();                                          // ready: click the drawing
}

change one thing and look

Things to try

Change one thing and compare the drawing.

  1. 01120 → 200

    The same drawing, 200 mm wide on paper. The canvas keeps the same size.

  2. 02a circle → a hatch

    Replace the circle with plot.hatch([[40, 40], [360, 40], [360, 210], [40, 210]], 6): a rectangle filled with parallel pen strokes.

  3. 03order → disorder

    Open the Molnár grid and change disorder from row / (n - 1) to 1: every row comes apart.

Supported shape calls record geometry for paper: shapes, lines and dots. translate(), rotate(), fill() and colours stay on the screen; paper uses the recorded coordinates and the pen in the holder.

put it on paper

On paper

Check the connection, paper and carriage position before starting.

  1. 01Chrome or Edge

    Connect the plotter by USB. These examples use Web Serial in Chrome or Edge on localhost or HTTPS.

  2. 02Home corner

    Park the carriage at the physical starting corner shown in Setup.

  3. 03Click your drawing

    Choose the plotter in the port list when the browser asks for it.

  4. 04Click again to stop

    The driver requests a stop, pen lift and motor release. Those commands need a working connection.

Direct plotting has been tested on the iDraw HSE / A2 with EBB firmware 3.0.2. Without hardware you can preview recorded shapes and use plot.engine.exportSVG(). Use the exported file with software that supports your machine.
